Our Services

Everything your office needs to run

Network Cabling & Structured Connectivity

A strong network starts with the right cabling infrastructure. We design and install structured cabling systems that support high-speed connectivity and long-term scalability.

  • CAT6 / CAT6a / Fibre installations
  • Network points for offices and workstations
  • Server room cabling and rack setup
  • Cable management and labelling
  • Network troubleshooting and upgrades
Electrical Installations & Office Power Solutions

Reliable power is critical to business operations. We provide safe and compliant electrical services tailored for commercial environments.

  • Office electrical wiring and distribution
  • DB board upgrades and installations
  • Backup power solutions (UPS & inverter-ready setups)
  • Lighting installations (including energy-efficient LED solutions)
  • Compliance with South African electrical standards
Boardroom & Smart Office Setup

Transform your meeting spaces into modern, collaborative environments with our professional boardroom solutions.

  • TV and display installations
  • Video conferencing systems (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet)
  • Audio systems and microphones
  • Wireless presentation solutions
  • Smart automation for lighting and presentations
CCTV Installation & Security Systems

Protect your business with intelligent surveillance and monitoring solutions designed for South African environments.

  • HD and IP CCTV camera installations
  • Remote monitoring via mobile and desktop
  • Access control and intercom systems
  • Motion detection and alert systems
  • Ongoing maintenance and support
Office Setup & Infrastructure Rollout

We handle full office setups from the ground up, ensuring everything is ready for your team to operate efficiently from day one.

  • New office infrastructure planning and implementation
  • Desk setups, network and power allocation
  • Server room and network cabinet installations
  • Relocations and infrastructure upgrades
