Cyber Security

Cyber Security Solutions by Mphoti Consulting

At Mphoti Consulting, we help organisations protect their systems, data, and digital platforms against evolving cyber threats. Our Cyber Security-as-a-Service offering provides a proactive, scalable, and business-focused approach to security; ensuring your organisation remains secure, compliant, and resilient without adding unnecessary complexity.

We partner with our clients to assess risks, strengthen defenses, and implement security solutions that integrate seamlessly into existing environments.

Our Services

What We Offer

01

Security Assessments & Vulnerability Management

Identify weaknesses before attackers do. We perform vulnerability assessments and security reviews to uncover risks across your applications, infrastructure, and networks.

02

Identity & Access Management

Ensure the right people have the right access. We implement secure authentication, authorization, and access control mechanisms to protect sensitive systems and data.

03

Application & Web Security

Protect your digital platforms from threats such as SQL injection, XSS, and other common vulnerabilities. We secure web applications, APIs, and platforms like AEM to ensure safe user interactions.

04

Security Monitoring & Incident Response

Detect and respond to threats in real time. We help you monitor systems, investigate incidents, and respond quickly to minimise impact.

05

Compliance & Governance

Align your business with industry standards and regulatory requirements. We assist with security policies, best practices, and frameworks to keep your organisation compliant.
